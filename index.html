Why We Built KeyPopKids

⌨️ Designed for desktop and laptop computers with a physical keyboard. It started with a 2-year-old who wanted to "help" every time we sat down at the computer. The problem was, there wasn't anything built for him to safely explore or enjoy on a keyboard. Every app was either too complicated, too restrictive, or just not fun enough for a toddler.

That's what led to KeyPopKids — a simple, interactive experience where kids can play, learn, and get comfortable using a keyboard at their own pace. No pressure, no wrong answers, just color, sound, and creativity every time they press a key.

What's inside
🎨

Generative Art

Every keypress paints something unique. Letters create petal bursts, numbers draw rings, special keys make starbursts. No two sessions ever look the same.

🔊

Letter Sounds

Every letter and number is spoken aloud the moment it's pressed, reinforcing recognition naturally and without pressure.

✏️

Letter Tracing

A large letter appears on screen and kids press the matching key to light it up. Progress dots guide them through the full alphabet.

📘

Word Mode

Simple words appear for kids to type letter by letter. Difficulty scales automatically from easy 3-letter words up to more challenging vocabulary.

🎉

Emoji Explosions

Hold any key and emojis rain across the screen. Pure chaos, completely loved by kids.

🏆

Badges & Milestones

14 badges to earn, from the very first keypress to typing every letter in the alphabet. Each one triggers a full-screen celebration.

🖼️

Downloadable Artwork

At the end of every session kids can save their generative masterpiece as a keepsake — with their name, the date, and session stats.

📊

Parent Dashboard

A PIN-protected view of session history, time played, favourite keys, a letter heatmap, words completed, letters traced, and badges earned.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2025

Overview

KeyPopKids is committed to protecting the privacy of all users, especially children. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect (if any), how we use it, and your rights.

Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information. KeyPopKids does not require you to create an account, provide your name, email address, or any other personal details to use the site.

All session data — including key press history, badges earned, and Parent Dashboard statistics — is stored locally on your device using your browser's localStorage. This data never leaves your device and is never transmitted to our servers or any third party.

Children's Privacy (COPPA)

KeyPopKids is designed for young children and is fully compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. No account creation is required. No data is collected, stored, or transmitted from child users.

Cookies & Tracking

KeyPopKids does not use tracking cookies or behavioral advertising. The only browser storage we use is localStorage to save your session stats locally on your own device.

Third-Party Services

KeyPopKids uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Fonts — for typography. Google may log font requests. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • CountAPI — a simple counter to display total global key presses. No personal data is sent.
  • Buy Me a Coffee — an optional support button. Only activated if you click it.

Sponsored Content

KeyPopKids may display sponsored content or advertising from time to time. Any sponsorships will be clearly labeled. We only work with family-friendly, child-safe sponsors.

Analytics

We may use privacy-friendly analytics tools to understand how visitors use the site. Any analytics we use will be configured to not collect personal data or use identifying cookies.

Contact
